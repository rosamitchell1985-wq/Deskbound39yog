Faster routes

Accessibility problem? Write “Accessibility” in the subject line and describe the page and the device. We aim to reply within two business days and to fix confirmed issues within thirty. See the accessibility statement.

Privacy request? Requests to know, delete, correct or opt out are handled through privacy@deskbound.co and described in the privacy policy.

Medical question? We cannot answer one. Not a clinician, not able to assess your back over email. Talk to a physician or a physical therapist — the disclaimer explains where that line sits.

Emergencies

This inbox is not monitored for emergencies. If you have severe pain, weakness, numbness in the groin area, or loss of bladder or bowel control, call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
