Cookie policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026.

This page lists what Deskbound stores in your browser, by category, and what each item is for. It covers cookies and also local storage, which is not technically a cookie but does the same job of remembering something on your device.

Cookie settings Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Category 1 — Strictly necessary

Set without asking, because the site cannot honor your choices without them. They contain no advertising identifier.

Category 2 — Preferences

Small conveniences for the timer. They stay on your device, are never transmitted, and are not used to identify you.

Category 3 — Advertising cookies

These are the ones the banner is about. They are set by third-party advertising vendors, including Google, and only after you accept. They are used to select ads, cap how often you see the same ad, measure performance, and detect invalid traffic. Some of them enable personalized advertising based on your visits to this and other sites.

Set byExamplesPurposeLifetime
Google (Ad Manager, AdSense)__gads, __gpi, IDE, test_cookieAd selection and delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and personalized advertising where permitted.Session up to about 13 months, set by Google

Google explains its advertising cookies and how partner sites use them at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can turn off personalized advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings, and opt out of many other vendors at aboutads.info or the NAI opt-out page.

What we do not use

  • No analytics cookies in this build. If we add analytics, this page changes first and the banner will cover it.
  • No social media pixels, no share-button trackers, no embedded video that phones home.
  • No fingerprinting, and no attempt to identify you across devices.
  • No cookie walls. Rejecting leaves the entire site working, including the timer.

Changing your mind

Open Cookie settings from any page footer to bring the banner back and choose again. To remove everything, clear cookies and site data for deskbound.co in your browser settings — on an iPhone, Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data, or in Chrome, Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data.

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a rejection and never show you the banner.

Consent management

For visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent management platform with TCF v2.3 support handles consent collection and passes the resulting string to ad vendors. The integration point for that script is marked in our JavaScript, and no advertising vendor is requested before it runs.
