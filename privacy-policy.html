Privacy policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026. Last updated: August 24, 2026.

Deskbound Media LLC (“Deskbound,” “we,” “us”) operates deskbound.co. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who else touches it, and the rights you have over it. It is written for a US audience and covers the state privacy laws that apply to us, not California alone.

The short version

  • We do not require an account and we do not sell your personal information for money.
  • The five-minute timer runs entirely in your browser. Your routine choices and sound setting are stored on your own device, not on a server.
  • Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, may set cookies and use identifiers to serve and measure ads. Under some state laws, that kind of cross-context advertising counts as “sharing” or “targeted advertising,” and you can opt out.
  • We honor the Global Privacy Control browser signal as an opt-out request.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signup: your email address, and the date you signed up.
  • Contact form or email: your name, email address, phone number if you choose to give one, the topic you select, and whatever you write in the message.

In the current build of this site, no form data is transmitted to a server — forms validate in the browser and stop there. When a mail backend is connected, this section will state the processor by name before the form goes live.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs kept by our hosting provider: IP address, user agent, referring page, the URL requested, and a timestamp. Used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Advertising and measurement identifiers set by third-party ad vendors if you accept cookies. See the cookie policy.

Information stored on your device, not sent to us

The site writes a small number of values to your browser's local storage: your cookie choice, the routine you last loaded, and whether you turned the timer sound on. These never leave your device and we cannot read them. Clearing your browser storage erases them.

Sensitive information

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, health information. Choosing a routine on this site is not a health record and is not transmitted anywhere. Do not describe a diagnosis in the contact form — we are not able to advise on it and would rather not hold it.

Why we use it

  • To send the weekly email you asked for, and to let you unsubscribe.
  • To answer messages you send us.
  • To keep the site up, fast and secure, and to diagnose problems.
  • To sell and measure advertising, which is how the site is paid for.
  • To meet legal obligations and to enforce our terms of use.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Advertising, and Google specifically

Deskbound is funded by advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info and the NAI opt-out page.
  • More on how Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Rejecting cookies on this site keeps every feature working. The timer, the routines and the reading experience are identical either way.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information for money. We disclose information only to:

  • Service providers that host the site, deliver email, or serve and measure advertising, under contracts limiting their use of it.
  • Advertising partners, in the form of online identifiers and device or usage data, when you have accepted advertising cookies. Some state laws classify this as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising or as “targeted advertising,” and you have the right to opt out of it.
  • Legal recipients, if required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A successor, if the site or the company is sold or merged. You would be notified here before your information became subject to a different policy.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live — California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws — you may have the right to:

  • Know and access: what categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the purpose, and the categories of third parties we disclose it to.
  • Delete: ask us to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Correct: ask us to fix inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing and of targeted advertising and profiling.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information, where a state law provides it. We do not collect sensitive personal information for the purposes that trigger this right.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not deny you service, charge a different price, or give you a lower quality experience for exercising any of these rights.
  • Appeal: if we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision. We will respond to an appeal within 45 days and, if we deny it again, tell you how to contact your state attorney general.

How to exercise them

Email privacy@deskbound.co with the request and the state you live in, or write to Deskbound Media LLC, 412 W Franklin St, Suite 210, Boise, ID 83702. You can also call (208) 555-0142. We respond within 45 days and may extend once by another 45 days if the request is complex, telling you first.

Because we do not maintain accounts, we verify requests by matching the email address you contact us from against the information we hold. If we cannot verify you, we will say so rather than guess. An authorized agent may submit a request with written permission from you, which we may ask to see.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Use the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link, available here and in the footer of every page. It records an opt-out for this browser immediately, and no advertising vendor is loaded on this device unless and until you change that choice. You can also change it any time from Cookie settings.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser or extension sends the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing for that browser, and we do not show you the cookie banner asking again. You do not need to do anything else.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, write to privacy@deskbound.co and we will delete it. We also do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

How long we keep things

  • Newsletter email addresses: until you unsubscribe, plus a short suppression record so we do not email you again by mistake.
  • Contact messages: up to 24 months, so we can pick up an old thread.
  • Server logs: typically 30 to 90 days, per our host's retention.
  • Advertising cookies: lifetimes set by each vendor, listed in the cookie policy.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the mailbox and any subscriber list is limited to the people who need it and protected by multi-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise.

Where information is processed

Deskbound is operated from the United States and information is processed here. If you are outside the US — including in the EEA, UK or Switzerland — you are using a US-based service and your information will be handled under US law. A Google-certified consent management platform handles consent for those regions where required.

Links to other sites

We link to public health and ergonomics resources such as OSHA, MedlinePlus, AAOS and the CDC. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we do not control them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the “last updated” date above and note the change at the top of this page for at least 30 days. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
